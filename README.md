## What is KovaFlow?

KovaFlow builds your trading journal **from your exchange data**, so you don't have to log trades by hand.

Connect an exchange, and KovaFlow:

1. **Collects your history**: fills, fees, funding, deposits, withdrawals and transfers.
2. **Rebuilds every trade**: entry, exit, duration, fees and R-multiple.
3. **Checks the result** against the balances your exchange reports.

And it is honest about gaps. If some of your history is missing, KovaFlow **shows the gap** instead of guessing what was there.

## Why it's different

Most journals quietly fill in what they don't know. KovaFlow doesn't.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🕳️ **Gaps are shown, not hidden** | Your equity curve breaks where data is missing. It never draws a line across a gap. |
| 🧾 **Every number shows its coverage** | You can always see whether a statistic is based on complete data or on part of it. |
| ⚖️ **Balances are checked** | Your trades are reconciled against what the exchange reports. Any difference is shown, never hidden. |
| 📅 **"No trades" ≠ "no data"** | On the calendar, a day you didn't trade and a day we couldn't load look different. |
| 🔒 **Read-only, always** | KovaFlow only reads. Where the exchange lets us check, keys that can trade or withdraw are refused. |
